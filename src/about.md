# About
<img src="/assets/images/about.jpg" alt="About image" class="about-image" />

I'm Mari, a product-minded operator and technical builder who likes turning complexity into something clear, useful, and workable.

I started my career in finance, where I developed a foundation in analysis, forecasting, and cross-functional problem solving. Curiosity about how technology gets built eventually pulled me into software engineering, where I went from analyzing systems to building them myself. I've since worked hands-on with modern web technologies and AI, building products from early ideas through implementation.

I'm most at home at the intersection of product, operations, and technology: bringing structure to ambiguity, improving how things work, connecting technical and nontechnical teams, and digging into a problem until I understand what actually needs to be solved. My technical background lets me speak comfortably with engineers while still thinking about the broader product, user, and business context.

Before any of that, I spent 12 years studying fine arts, which probably explains why I've always been drawn to both analytical and creative problems.

Outside of work, I'm usually looking for a different kind of challenge. I ski, scuba dive, run, hike, and generally have a hard time resisting an adventure.
